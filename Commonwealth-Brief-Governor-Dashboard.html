Definition & calculation

The source counts residential addresses: 3,107,864 in November 2025 minus 3,073,303 in November 2024 equals 34,561. Progress is 34,561 ÷ 222,000 = 15.6%, rounded. The remainder is 222,000 − 34,561 = 187,439. This corrects an inconsistent remainder in the original page; it is not a 2026 update.

How to read this edition

Snapshot, not live: redesigning the page does not refresh the underlying reporting. Public announcements, preliminary estimates, historical appropriations, forecasts and calculated values are labeled separately. Source websites may change after publication.

Housing arithmetic: 222,000 − 34,561 = 187,439 remaining. The progress figure is 15.6%, rounded from the same inputs. The source page and original briefing carried an inconsistent remainder; this edition uses the explicit calculation.

Comparability: July payroll figures come from the BLS table; the June sector table remains dated June. The 58.6% opioid-death reduction is calculated from 2,364 in 2022 and 978 in 2025. No U.S. unemployment comparison or cross-population overdose ratio is inferred.

Staff prompts: these are suggested questions, not official decisions, assignments, approved risk ratings or completed actions. Current public-safety alerts, operational feeds, constituent information and the Governor's calendar are not connected.
